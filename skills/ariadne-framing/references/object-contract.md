## Domain Admission and Its Limit

Structural explanation admits a natural process, technical system, ecological process, human action, organization, or institution only when it can leave comparable material and the analyst can declare the seven candidate fields: boundary, state variables, relations or transitions, interfaces, time window, scale profile, and identity criterion. Purely metaphysical assertions, objects that can offer no empirical field, and systems that stand only on naming or narrative coherence do not enter empirical judgment. They may still be discussed, and may be registered as D-type definitions; they never borrow this framework's empirical conclusion grades. The human-structured world is a specialized domain that adds human clauses, normative commitments, and authorization procedures; its purposes are never projected back onto non-human processes, and "is" never yields "ought" (§2 opening).

The admission gate governs only strong claims made in this framework's name: G empirical question families and the C and CM claims that depend on them. It is not a universal truth gate. Existing studies, archives, oral accounts, archaeology, original texts, formal proofs, textual interpretation, and normative argument keep their own standing and support what their actual design supports. Keep their design type (preregistered confirmation, post hoc discovery, exploration, historical reconstruction) as it was; never back-fill a pre-result contract, and never strip their content because it was not converted into a G-instance (§1.3, §2.1, §2.1.1). When an adequate domain method already answers the question, the frame may add only source and boundary checks.

## Two-Stage Admission

1. Declare a candidate object as `S*=<B,X,R,I,T,SP,K>`. Completing every field proves only that the question is stated; an incomplete declaration supports exploratory questions only.
2. Send any claim that the grouping is useful, that a named channel has an effect, that history adds conditions beyond the current representation, or that a scale move closes, to an evidence-bearing G-instance owned by `ariadne-evidence`.

Do not place a zero model in the candidate object; freeze a complexity-matched `N0` only inside the concrete instance that tests a specific claim. Keep four states apart: candidate object registered, positive G claim unsupported, zero model supported, object granted bounded positive support. A failed positive gate never supports `N0` by itself.

## Outputs, Tasks, and Request Classification

The source names four outputs, each with its own boundary (§1.1): explanation does not acquire forecasting standing or disposal power; derivation keeps branches, unknowns, residuals, and stop conditions; conditional forecasting registers target and baseline before the result and checks and writes back after it; finite choice compares action, delay, probing, exit, and no action under norms, protection, evidence, authorization, and action ceilings, and may form an analytic recommendation for deliberation while real adoption still needs protection, review, and lawful authorization. Four tasks cut across them (§1.4): explanation compares competing accounts; diagnosis locates constraints, unstable links, and capacity gaps under a declared object, scale, window, and risk tier, and a found risk never authorizes others to act on the analyzed person or strips their standing to object; normative choice states value premises, rights claims, consequences, and conflict reasons without rewriting a value choice as factual necessity; intervention declares level, authorization source, scope, stop condition, and remedy, and checks reversibility from the affected positions, because a technical rollback does not erase another person's loss.

| Method Record `request_type` | Requested work | Framing boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `explanation` | Explain an observed or reported phenomenon | Do not predict or prescribe |
| `diagnosis` | Locate constraints, instability, or capacity gaps | Report only the requested diagnostic target; no handling power |
| `dynamic_derivation` | Expand conditional event paths | Preserve branches, residuals, unknowns, and stops |
| `conditional_forecast` | Make a target-and-deadline future claim | Require a later frozen forecast registry |
| `finite_choice` | Compare action, delay, probing, exit, and no action | Require explicit norms; an analytic recommendation is for deliberation only |
| `intervention_review` | Review a proposed real-world intervention | Require protection, stopping, rollback, and remedy later |
| `governance_review` | Review rules, public claims, or methodology governance | Require challenge, appeal, downgrade, and retirement later |
| `synthesis_design` | Produce a design or plan whose core is a judgment | Constraints and comparison only; construction and adoption return to the host |

If one request contains several rows, split it or declare an explicit order. An explanation does not inherit forecast status, a forecast does not inherit authorization, and an opinion, option comparison, or draft rule does not by itself acquire power to bind others. When such an output is in fact used for handling, eligibility, resources, or another high-impact decision, review it by that foreseeable use; the label "suggestion" never lowers responsibility. Judge results item by item: a real local improvement stays recorded while other outcomes need their own evidence.

## Task Profile and Representation

Method constraint 法1 requires the task and the representation to be declared together (§4.17). Every run freezes in `object_contract.task_profile`:

| Field | Freeze | Typical failure when skipped |
|---|---|---|
| `target_query` | The quantity, classification, or decision the answer must deliver | A summary that answers a nearby but different question |
| `horizon` | The period the answer must hold for | A static fit read as a dynamic forecast |
| `environment` | The conditions under which the answer is claimed | An answer extrapolated into a different regime |
| `allowed_operations` | Observation only, or the actions whose effects must be evaluable | A representation that cannot evaluate an allowed intervention |
| `error_tolerance` | The loss or error the use can bear | Precision claims the material cannot carry |

Sufficiency is checked separately for static recovery (can the registered representation answer the target query now), dynamic closure (can it advance itself over the horizon without the dropped detail), and intervention sufficiency (does closure survive the allowed operations). A total can answer a total question yet fail to identify local overload; a representation that closes under symmetric service can fail once an operation binds capacity at one position. Representations also differ in recoverability of the source state, cost of computation, and the error they carry, so name which of these the task needs (§4.18–4.19, Appendix C.9). When the target query, operation, object, or window changes, re-declare the profile instead of reusing earlier sufficiency.

## Candidate Object Fields

| Field | Freeze | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|
| `boundary` / B | Included and excluded states, relations, locations, actors, or cases | Closure, naturalness, or moral membership |
| `state_variables` / X | Task-relevant registered variables and their observation positions | The object's complete state |
| `relations_or_transitions` / R | Endpoints, direction, timing, and applicability | Causality from adjacency or correlation |
| `interfaces` / I | Inputs, outputs, filters, delays, conversions, or blocks | Control, consent, or responsibility |
| `time_window` / T | Observation, explanation, forecast, or intervention window | Persistence outside the window |
| `scale_profile` / SP | A, X, T, O, C, R, I, N, and J positions | Transfer between axes or scales |
| `identity_criterion` / K | Changes compatible with retaining the same object | Value, health, maturity, or a duty to persist |
| `task_profile` | Target query, horizon, environment, allowed operations, error tolerance | Sufficiency for any other task |
| `status` | `candidate` or `incomplete` during framing | `supported` without an eligible evidence instance |

Each use also declares the region or environment, the material window, the extrapolation unit, and the exclusions (§2 opening).

## Groups and Circles

A circle is first a candidate grouping, not a natural entity established by naming. State members, roles, channels, entry and exit conditions, overlap basis, time window, identity criterion, and the residual that motivates the grouping. Documents, relations, and exposure records can support object facts directly. A claim that grouping adds explanatory or forecasting gain is compared against no grouping and a single-circle baseline; formal G1 support does not replace other grounds for an object's existence. Parallel, nested, and overlapping relations are judged on declared dimensions; potential bridges, actual transmission, and transmission effects need separate evidence. A newly added circle names the necessary question it answers, the distinction lost without it, and its measurement and privacy cost; otherwise keep it as an environmental condition or variable candidate. Keep an object without proving predictive gain when it is needed to describe established responsibility, membership differences, or matter-specific authority (§2.10).

## Proposition Roles and the Minimal Action Bridge

Split every mixed sentence before registering it (§2.2):

- **D** definition: fixes meaning; carries no empirical truth.
- **G** empirical question family: G1 grouping gain, G2 named channel, G3 historical condition increment, G4 representation closure; a concrete instance is a factual claim with object, target, conditions, comparison, and failure mode. Whether the fact holds and whether the evidence suffices are different questions.
- **E** epistemic constraint; **H** human-domain clause, a fit marker rather than a law of human nature; **N** normative commitment; **O** operating procedure.
- **F** conditional formal proposition: a necessary relation inside a stated model (F1–F6, owned by `ariadne-scale`), proved rather than observed.
- **C** is not an eighth kind of fact: it is a use contract for registered results and mechanism-condition combinations; C12 bridges to normative procedure.

The minimal bridge to a real action is: descriptive or explanatory conclusion + explicit normative premise + current evidence + lawful authorization + action ceiling + stop or withdrawal condition. With any part missing, real handling stops at description, candidate explanation, further evidence, no action, or a reversible probing proposal. An analytic recommendation for deliberation is not handling and is governed by `ariadne-choice`.

## State, Structure, and Locked Words

Use these terms only with their declared tests:

- **Change:** a detectable difference in a registered variable; no implied cause, direction, or persistence.
- **Operation:** state transitions while B, R, I, SP, and K remain valid. **Persistence:** K continues within T; no implied health, legitimacy, or desirability.
- **Evolution:** a contract field changes beyond a declared fluctuation window; no implied progress, selection, or path dependence. **Dissolution:** the prior K no longer holds; classify termination or conversion separately.
- **Effective feedback:** a prior state or output returns through a traceable channel and detectably changes a later state, probability, timing, or constraint relative to no return. Receipt or logging is not feedback, and effective feedback is not stability.
- **Cost** names quantity type, unit, window, accounting boundary, and bearing position; only like quantities, or unlike ones with an explicit conversion or accounting mapping, can be traced as transferred. **Externality** first needs a cross-boundary causal channel and distribution positions, then an institutional or accounting rule that excludes it from a decision's accounting; otherwise say spillover or accounting gap. Recording an evidenced cross-boundary harm does not wait for a legal-liability finding.
- **Repair** requires a target identity criterion K* or function criterion F* declared before the result; it is neither restoring the historical original nor value improvement, and who decides and who may implement pass through N and O. **Selection** keeps system filtering, actor choice, and governance choice apart. **Responsibility and authorization** register carrier, like-cost bearer, beneficiary, stop right, and responsible subject separately; execution, greatest ability, greatest exposure, visibility, or knowledge never alone yields responsibility, and wider observation or higher confidence never widens disposal power (§2.8).
- **Scale transformation**, **circle-relation transformation**, and **representation or wording translation** are three different moves (§5.10.1); record which one the frame anticipates in `scale_scope.transform_kind` and route its audit to `ariadne-scale`.

## Minimum Structural Closure

| Primitive | Framing question | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| U01 candidate object | Can all D0 fields be stated? | Does not prove G1 |
| U02 boundary and interface | What is inside, outside, filtered, delayed, or blocked? | Boundary is not closure or control |
| U03 state and observable | What is registered, from which measurement position, with what error? | Observable is not the whole state |
| U04 component, carrier, substrate | What composes, carries, or enables the process? | Carrier is not a responsible subject |
| U05 relation and constraint | What repeatably narrows feasible states or transitions? | Relation is not causality or legitimacy |
| U06 flow, channel, transmission | What moves, where, at what rate and loss? | A named channel is not an evidenced effect |
| U07 feedback and update | What returns and which later field changes? | Feedback is not learning, repair, or stability |
| U08 condition and environment | Which outside variable acts through which interface? | Environment is not a total cause |
| U09 load, capacity, recovery margin | What comparable demand and capacity share units, window, and location? | Overload is not cumulative damage or duty |
| U10 trace, memory, path | What persists and re-enters later transitions? | A trace is not path dependence |
| U11 phase, transition, dissolution | Which criteria distinguish regimes under the same K? | A phase is not a maturity rank |

Mark a primitive `not_applicable` with a reason when the current object has no lawful counterpart. Otherwise keep missing content visible.

## Connection Needs

Structural explanation turns on which judgments can be joined under which conditions (§1.11). Aggregate material is not enough to answer a distribution question; two local causal edges that each hold are not thereby carried by the same cases; options feasible one at a time may not share finite resources at once; a lawfully authorized action has not thereby reached its endpoint. At framing, list the joins the task will need and their likely breakpoint type: representation, identification, clock, capacity, rule, material reach, or authority. Do not file a break under generic complexity. Later modules keep each join's input, output, condition, and failure mode, revise the actual breakpoint (add the minimal distinction, correct the rule, change access or decision timing; resource or authority gaps are not fixed by more interpretive precision), and keep the finite results already earned upstream.

## Missing States and Per-Proposition Downgrade

Use one of four explicit prefixes in string-valued Method Record fields and unknown lists (§2.9):

- `unknown:` knowable in principle, currently unsupported.
- `not_applicable:` no lawful counterpart in the current contract, with reason.
- `not_observable:` unavailable through the current channel or window; never rewritten as "does not exist".
- `withheld_for_protection:` available but not disclosed; state basis, minimum scope, duration, and trusted review path.

When a critical variable is unknown, a non-applicability condition is unhandled, or a core channel cannot be observed or credibly reviewed, the affected conclusion steps down from candidate hypothesis to question list to description only. Downgrade proposition by proposition: register each claim's dependencies in `depends_on`, lower only the claims that rest on the gap, and keep independently supported claims at their level. Only a failed global source, shared premise, or object boundary blocks every related output. Renaming, splitting, or opening another branch never restores a ceiling. Unclear authorization, irreversible harm, unreachable appeal or stop, or professional overreach suspends real handling and moves it to the proper procedure. A downgrade is not a failure.

## Strong-Judgment and Protection Screen

Before handing off a high-impact frame, ask the ten questions (§1.6):

1. Is the candidate object established rather than merely named?
2. What evidence supports the claim?
3. Is there a causal bridge?
4. Has scale been changed or collapsed?
5. Which competing explanations remain?
6. Whose rights or protected interests may be affected?
7. What is the actual authorization and jurisdiction?
8. Can the proposed process be stopped?
9. Can harms be remedied or rolled back?
10. Can affected parties challenge or appeal?

They are not a scoring list. When one cannot be answered, name what it limits (factual judgment, mechanism account, extrapolation range, publication standing, or real action) and downgrade or stop proportionately. Preserve subject status, dissent channels, exit where possible, minimum harm, traceable responsibility, and genuine rollback as the protection floor (§1.7). Inability to exit, difficulty expressing, or unwillingness to disclose is not consent, absence of loss, or absence of objection. Broad observation, influence, rank, or technical ability never expands J authorization. Whether the run is a strong judgment is set by actual and foreseeable use, as `ariadne-skill/references/routing.md` defines.

## Method Record Mapping

- Put the seven frozen object fields, the task profile, and status in `object_contract`.
- Put the material cutoff in `evidence_cutoff`; included material is not empirical evidence merely because it is listed.
- Summarize source and target positions and the anticipated transform kind in `scale_scope`; use `transform_kind: none` with `transform_status: not_needed` when no transformation is claimed.
- Put definitions and framed claims in `claim_ledger` with D/G/E/H/N/O/F types and `depends_on` where a claim rests on another.
- Put unresolved fields, connection needs, protection gaps, and strong-judgment gaps in `unknowns_and_counterevidence`.
- Add `ariadne-framing` to `module_runs` and route only applicable next modules.
- Use `output_ceiling: description_only` for standalone framing and `authorization_effect: none` in every run.

## Strong Example

Request: "Incident reports rose after a school changed its reporting app. Should the district install cameras everywhere?"

Frame the reporting question before the intervention question. Do not equate reported incidents with incident occurrence: fewer or more records can come from real change, changed detection, changed reporting entry, or record handling. Do not treat the proposed camera system as already inside the observed object.
