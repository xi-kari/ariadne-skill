# Human-Domain Contracts

Section anchors (§) point to the source text named in `ariadne-skill/references/source-navigation.json`.

## Domain and Method Record Boundary

Use the H-World adapter only after declaring a D0 object, task, and scale. The general primitives describe broad empirical structure; the human adapter alone handles intention, meaning, power, institutions, authorization, responsibility, and value. HV01-HV11 and U01-U11 are managed separately: similar names, equal counts, or neighboring functions never make them one-to-one equivalent, and human-specific judgments never flow back as laws of nature (§7 opening). Do not project human properties onto technology, crises, metrics, platforms, resources, or nonhuman systems merely because they affect people, and do not make a person, group, or dissenter a mechanism, source, or responsible subject because they are visible, disagree, or stand near a disturbance.

Interfaces and types carry no empirical truth of their own. Formal support comes from G-instances or H1, H4, and H5-instances. **Existing domain research supports bounded conclusions according to its own design and is not withdrawn for lacking this framework's instance status**; H2, H3, H6, interfaces, and types never pose as empirical support through complete fields or successful classification. Filling a card does not require unlimited collection of personal data, and it is never a precondition for ordinary understanding, help-seeking, or lawful service (§7.3).

For a standalone run, create the common Method Record and record:

- the actor, circle, or joint object, its `B/X/R/I/T/SP/K`, and the task profile in `object_contract`;
- only the source cutoff timestamp, admitted source IDs, and after-cutoff exclusion flag in `evidence_cutoff`;
- source scale, target scale, transform kind, and transformation status in `scale_scope`;
- observed behavior, candidate variables, H-instance state, HV route, circle relations, and bounded conclusions in `claim_ledger`, with `depends_on` where a conclusion rests on another;
- missing states, refusals, conflicts, counterexamples, local exclusion zones, and competing explanations in `unknowns_and_counterevidence`;
- observation position, privacy state, allowed purpose, at least two distinct alternatives, and responsible human review in the typed `ariadne-human` module payload;
- `affected_positions` as a closed four-class object: `direct`, `indirect`, `low_power`, and `no_exit`; every class contains `status`, `positions`, and `reason`, and an unknown, protected, or non-applicable class remains explicit with an empty list rather than collapsing into free text;
- the ceiling transition, traceable module input/output records, necessary handoffs, and `authorization_effect: none`.

Create a child record rather than overwriting a parent when the object, evidence, permitted use, identity, task, or scale changes. Do not let a fuller form raise the empirical or action status of its contents.

## H1-H6 Role Boundary

Three empirical hypotheses, two operating or classification rules, and one normative boundary (§7.1):

| Claim | Role | Permit | Stop before |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 meaning and coordination | Empirical hypothesis requiring an eligible H1-instance | Register a conditional anchor only for one preregistered resource-allocation, action-choice, or coordination result | Shared inner life, genuine consent, common will, or enforced unity from slogans, shared language, or interpreter projection |
| H2 carrying and responsibility separation | Operating rule | Separate carrying vehicle, same-type cost bearer, beneficiary, stop right, and responsible subject | Treating capacity as duty, visibility as responsibility, or classification as a reproduction mechanism |
| H3 institutional writeback | Classification rule | Separate receipt, field change, actual execution, effective writeback, and duration | Treating intake as writeback, one writeback as learning, or an update as lasting repair |
| H4 power, mediation, and reflexivity | Empirical hypothesis requiring an eligible H4-instance | Register one preregistered effect on evidence coverage, expression safety, object behavior, or reflexive response through a specified channel | Inferring malice, consent from silence, automatic remedy, or a mechanism from a "power", "platform", "metric", or "AI" label |
| H5 historical trace carrier | Empirical hypothesis requiring an eligible H5-instance | Register a persistent trace in one preregistered carrier family, one matching atomic carrier, and one persistence criterion | Treating a trace as a path effect: later effects through a current carrier follow the carried route, and an increment beyond the current representation needs G3 |
| H6 open-ended bearing | Normative boundary | Describe real cost, voluntariness, direction, alternatives, structural consequences, and stop rights | Diagnosing whether someone "has love", treating sacrifice as proof, or conscripting continued unilateral bearing |

Use the same two-stage, version-frozen, append-only evidence discipline for H1, H4, and H5-instances as for G-instances (owned by `ariadne-evidence`). Accept only `supported`, `unsupported_or_undecided`, `null_supported`, or `not_evaluated`. A failed positive rule is not a supported null. H1-H6 generate only empirical descriptions, classification results, evidence gaps, and normative needs; real adjustment always passes C12, runtime explicit N premises, J authorization, and O procedures.

## Strong Types

Type separation splits follow-up questions, required evidence, and responsibility boundaries (§7.2). Keep each type independently evidenced. One record may support several distinguishable facts, but its source dependence stays visible and it is never counted as several independent confirmations; one case may hold several types, and evidence for one never closes another.

| Type | Ask and minimum evidence | Do not collapse into |
|---|---|---|
| GC generation condition | What resource, technical, institutional, relational, trauma, crisis, or opportunity condition made generation possible or more likely? Record appearance, change, or removal times and actual exposure; "made more likely" needs comparison, outcome difference, a candidate channel, and competitors. | GS, GE, intention, or responsibility; observing a condition does not show who the subject is or that generation happened |
| GS generating subject | Who could understand and perform initiation, translation, or transfer? Check identity, capacity, resources, actual action, and authorization status separately. | Technology, crisis, platform, metric, resource window, an inference backward from the event, or the whole responsibility set |
| GE generation event | What state transition formed, and when? Compare pre-event state, time point, post-event state, and criterion under one measurement contract. | A personified event or an invented subject for emergence; an unidentified mechanism does not mean the event did not happen |
| CV carrying vehicle | Who or what actually executes, transmits, maintains, records, cares, or repairs (people, posts, procedures, facilities, technical systems)? Use work logs, resource and capacity flow, cost, stop rights, and function after substitution. | RS; whether an organization can bear responsibility is judged by its standing and specific rules, never by grouping it with equipment |
| RS responsible subject | Who bears what responsibility on what basis? Separate conduct, decision, authorization, supervision, benefit, and remedy, with knowledge, control, scope, period, appeal, and remedy evidence. | The most visible actor, the person who actually carried the work, or the person harmed; a low-authority executor is never the default main responsible party for being visible |
| IS instability source | Where did a disturbance enter or accumulate? Locate first appearance or accumulation, entry point, timing, exposure difference, and alternatives. | Malice, IM, all responsibility, or dissenters as a source by identity |
| IM instability mechanism | Through what causal path did the disturbance alter state, boundary, feedback, rhythm, or direction? Trace intermediates, timing, bridge evidence, failure conditions, and competitors. | A person, group, or dissenter named as the mechanism |
| IB instability behavior | What action, inaction, or output was observed? Preserve actor or carrier, context, scale, window, raw records, logs, and multi-source observation. | IS, IM, malice, or total responsibility; silence and absence are split into did-not-happen, unobservable, and protectively withheld |

Apply IS, IM, and IB across HV02, HV06, HV07, HV08, HV09, and HV10.

| C scale and object | `scale_profile`, `effective_object`, `scale_invariants`, `required_scale_additions`, `changing_semantics`, `non_applicable_objects`, `forbidden_elevation` | Register `SP=<A,X,T,O,C,R,I,N,J>`, the object, what must remain, what must be added on scale change, what changes meaning, where the interface does not apply, and which aggregation is forbidden. |

| E carrying, norms, and correction | `carrier`, `responsible_subject`, `normative_status`, `judgment_ceiling`, `action_ceiling`, `counterexamples`, `appeal`, `rollback` | Separate implementation from responsibility, state the non-self-authorizing normative status, cap judgment and action, retain counterexamples, and bind real appeal and rollback procedures. |

Keep the four dependency relations disjoint:

- Put only the formal prerequisites that every conclusion level needs in `inferential_requires`.
- Put only the gates every level must pass in `protocol_requires`: E gates, CAUSAL, EVIDENCE, ANALOGY, SOURCE, or explicit N/O gates.
- Put H2, H3, H6 and any definition, classification rule, or normative boundary the interface refines in `specializes`.
- Put only the object to which the interface applies in `applies_to`.

Conditions needed only at one level go inside `conditional_support_routes`, each with `route_id`, `claim_level`, `when`, `additional_inferential_requires`, `additional_protocol_requires`, `allowed_conclusion`, and `result_ceiling`. Candidate description, classification, conditional effect, mechanism explanation, and intertemporal explanation are separate levels: **a lower level is never sealed off because a higher level's premise is missing, and a higher level never borrows a lower level's complete fields**. Every card now has an R0 route that does not depend on higher-tier premises such as G1, H1, H4, or G2. G-instances, H-instances, human variables, and CM contracts appear only as inferential prerequisites, never disguised as protocol gates. An empty dependency (`无（空集合）` in the card source) only means no object is registered for that dependency type; it is not evidence. Read one route as a whole and never combine favorable fields from different routes after seeing a result. Use qualified IDs such as `human_variable:HV03`, `human_strong_type:RS`, `universal_primitive:U07`, `scale_axis:J`, and explicit `O1`-`O4`; never a bare `O`. Scale axis `N` means network topology; the "runtime explicit N premise" in every card's `action_ceiling` refers to the normative gates N1-N5 owned by `ariadne-choice`.

### Variable-Specific Distinctions

- **Meaning forms (HV03).** Meaning does not act only as private belief: names, narratives, rituals, promises, and classifications provide publicly recognizable forms of action (asking for help, refusing, contracting, mourning, contesting standing). Answer separately how participants understand the form in context, who may give an interpretation with institutional consequences, and which action or resource change actually runs through that interpretation. An interpretation can be reasonable while its causal effect is open, and coordination can happen without shared ultimate beliefs. When translating concepts across traditions, keep the question the concept answered in its original practice, who held interpretive authority, its obligations, and its disputes; religious identity, normative source, legal standing, and personal belief may share one translated word while performing different functions. Comparisons can be partly comparable, locally unalignable, or short of material; judge task-relevant translation loss against both the analyst's task and the affected people's practical questions (an administrative count may not say who can draw water, inherit, speak, or refuse).
- **Care management labor (HV05).** Separate doing a task from recognizing needs, scheduling, reminding, checking, and standing by. Someone else completing one task is real help but does not show that all management passed over. The substitute's resources, support, and refusal conditions enter the same evaluation. High burden can come from shared scarcity and is not evidence of malicious conscription; existing duty scope, term, handover responsibility, and the safety of anyone harmed are argued separately.
- **Review quality (HV07).** Review quality is not defined by reversal counts. Upholding a decision with reasons can fulfill procedural responsibility and improve explanations, records, and later handling. Without a change in the target state it is not effective writeback for that target, but listening and fair procedure keep their value; a wrong reversal can be writeback without being correction.
- **Power beyond masking (HV08).** Eligibility divisions, examinations and files, task training, spatial arrangements, and rewards and sanctions can change which options are feasible, which abilities are recognized, what counts as normal, and how actors understand themselves. Trace category proposal, institutional adoption, resource or opportunity allocation, participants' responses, and later feedback step by step: a category existing is not its being applied, and being applied is not everyone accepting it. One person may accept, use strategically, conceal, reinterpret, or refuse an identity. Never infer endorsement from public compliance, and never prejudge every classification as false or harmful. Capability, communication, legal effect, legitimacy belief, and normative legitimacy are evidenced separately. "Field" is a working name and presumes no common unit, conserved quantity, potential function, or additive force.
- **Burden units (HV09).** Task time, simultaneous activity, standby, cognitive management, sleep loss, and future opportunity have different units and overlap; say which add, which double count, and never universalize "entropy" into an additive entity. An average is not each bearer's actual load. Relief in one place may move burden to other households, posts, or future periods, so conclude only about the net range asked.
- **Open-ended bearing (HV11).** It is a bounded category for examining a specific action, not a definition of love, a detector, or a personality rank. Easy companionship, the concern of people with limited capacity, unsuccessful creation, private mourning, and refusals that produced no new structure lose no experience or value for lacking high cost or structural effect. High cost, lasting effort, or a kind self-report does not prove free consent, fair distribution, or a legitimate duty. Thin material lowers external naming strength without removing the person's standing to be heard, protected, and to refuse.

## Elevation, Evidence, and Action Ceilings

Compare scale moves axis by axis and state what is kept, changed, lost, which positions are low-visibility, and which local exclusion zones exist (§7.4). Six elevations are forbidden: individual experience directly representing a group state; a local mechanism directly representing the whole; member bearing directly becoming a group duty; carrier function directly becoming responsibility; behavior directly becoming source or mechanism; and a stage or load directly becoming a moral rank. Judgment level stays at or below evidence coverage; action level stays at or below J authorization.

## Actor-Circle Mutual Constitution

People are not passive components of circles, and circles are not personality shells (§7.5). Register both directions: circles change actors through resources, information, roles, rewards and sanctions, reputation, identity, and exit conditions; actors change circles through labor, expression, refusal, alliance, exit, bridging, and institutional writeback.

| Interface | Record | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|
| Entry and role | Membership basis, role, and participation | Membership is endorsement |
| Resources and load | Shared pressure and each position's capacity | Shared pressure produces one response |
| Meaning and identity | Public narratives and heterogeneous expression | A public narrative speaks for every member |
| Feedback and writeback | Return channel and changed field | Being received is effective writeback |

Personality is only a slow-variable candidate of an actor; S0-S6 are only collective prototypes; neither is applied to the other. Individual material enters circle judgments only through explicit aggregation, representation, propagation, or institutional mechanisms.

## Practice and Institutional Reproduction

Actors keep interpreting and invoking rules, allocating resources, handling exceptions, passing on skills, and confirming eligibility (§7.6). Separate written rules, public claims, daily working rules, and actual consequences, and record independently when practice changed while documents did not, or documents changed while practice continued. Formal writeback follows H3; everyday institutionalization and habit formation are not one writeback.

To analyze one round of change, reconstruct separately the earlier rules and resource relations, actors' projects and option sets, the ensuing interactions, and the arrangements later kept, reorganized, or interrupted. A later round inherits earlier results, but later success is never written back as an original condition, and sequence alone never establishes causation. Skilled knowledge may not be fully sayable; reflection and innovation are limited by time, resources, power, and others' responses. Generation and reproduction allow competing mechanisms: compliance may come from recognition, dependence, sanction risk, or custom; similar organizational forms may come from technical effectiveness, coercive requirements, professional training, or imitation. Each explanation names the process material that would distinguish it and its failure condition. Individual action does not vanish because it is constrained, and a structural explanation does not automatically excuse someone with knowledge and real choice.

## Actor State and Personality Boundary

Represent an actor as (§9.1):

`A*=<actor_id, observation_window, circle_memberships, roles, slow_variables, medium_variables, fast_variables, material_conditions, experience_meaning_conditions, unknowns, evidence_state, privacy_and_allowed_use>`.

The star marks a candidate snapshot. A name, occupation, identity, or single act never fills the state. An actor ID grants no representation of the person's whole life; membership is not endorsement; a role is not actual capacity or real will; state variables are not a stable essence; conditions are not a single cause; unknowns are never freely imputed.

| Time band | Candidate content | Evidence and update rule | Misuse to block |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slow | Long-term value ordering, habitual interpretation, long-term capacities and resource position, identity commitments, trust or attachment tendencies | "Slow" means relatively slow in this window only. Persistence needs cross-time evidence and cross-context stability needs coverage of different contexts; scale validity is not observation of every setting; a tendency seen only in one high-pressure circle keeps "activated by that circle" as a competitor; major resource, relationship, learning, or institutional changes update, split, or retire the variable | Innate essence, true self, or universal trait |
| Medium | Role identity, phase goals, relationship expectations, alliance position, accumulated fatigue, recent reputation and trust | The main interface of mutual constitution between person and circle; looking at one side only turns structural pressure into a personal defect or dissolves personal choice into environment | Role reaction rewritten as personality |
| Fast | Immediate emotion, arousal, attention, fear, impulse, current belief, short-term bodily state, perceived opportunity, current strategy | Bind to a time point and event; may enter medium or slow variables through thresholds, public traces, irreversible actions, or others' feedback, and the derivation must write the channel | One emotion or act predicts the person's future; "they were always like that" read back from the outcome |

**Learned dispositions** form through repeated practice in families, schools, work, and peer groups (who had time to practice, who was corrected or encouraged, which ways of speaking, postures, and judgments were rewarded). They differ from a one-off strategic choice and from a personality essence. One action never reveals family origin or a whole life history. One actor may carry several socializations, and an old disposition can misfit a new environment. **Group average associations never fill an individual state, and departing from a group norm is not abnormality** (§9.2).

**Personality is only a conditional slow-variable hypothesis** compressing cross-time, cross-context behavioral differences (§9.3). A personality hypothesis card states a distinguishable, refutable description without insulting or pathologizing labels; behavioral indicators; observed and unobserved contexts; competing explanations (role demands, short-term emotion, material constraints, strategy, cultural norms, measurement bias); counterevidence; status (observation record, supported hypothesis, candidate, disputed, unknown, retired); allowed uses (research, communication, scenario derivation, and similar limited uses); and prohibited uses (clinical diagnosis, essence adjudication, discriminatory screening, automatic authorization or punishment). Never perform clinical or psychiatric diagnosis or unauthorized psychological assessment; an external professional diagnosis enters only as sourced, authorized material within its stated boundary, and is never generated, expanded, or repurposed by the framework.

**Role activation** (§9.4). Entering a different circle can change at least six conditions: visible information, available resources, rewards and sanctions, responsibility, identity activation, and exit capacity. Treat those conditions, independently measured individual differences, and their interaction as competitors. Checking easily changed situations first does not mean assigning all shared explained variance to the situation in advance. Compare individual-difference, situational, and interaction models under the same target, data split, available information, complexity, and measurement budget. A personality indicator that adds out-of-window predictive gain supports a bounded prediction, not a main causal mechanism, an innate essence, or an intervention priority. When a person always appears with one situation, repeated observation cannot separate their contributions. A role-activation record answers which event activated which role; whether the role comes from formal rules, informal expectation, self-commitment, or threat; whether the person endorses it; conflicts with other roles; what it allows, forbids, and costs; and exit conditions. Cross-context stability states whether it concerns rank order, mean level, the distribution of state fluctuation, or the profile of conditional responses: a change in exposure to situations can move a mean while conditional responses stay the same, and a random intercept in a statistical model is undecomposed individual difference, never a personality by name.

Split two-way influence into two testable chains and their joint loop (§9.5): circle to person (a rule or event changes information, resources, risk, role, or meaning, which through perception and capacity changes fast variables, then through repeated feedback, public traces, or relationship restructuring enters medium or slow variables); person to circle (expression, labor, refusal, exit, bridging, alliance, or innovation changes resource flows, role allocation, shared interpretation, member boundaries, institutional records, or external interfaces). Only a return channel, delay, threshold, and trace closes the loop; a signal that arrives without changing later state is a counterexample. "They influence each other" is not a completed explanation.

Evidence status moves conservatively: unknown, candidate, supported hypothesis; observation records; disputed; retired (§9.6). Self-report, others' reports, behavioral observation, and scales each carry their own biases; multi-source agreement does not exclude shared environment, shared narrative, or shared measurement bias. A variable proposed after seeing an outcome can help explain and design the next test but never supports the earlier prediction. A changed definition, time band, or indicator creates a new version and does not overwrite old failures.

**Material and experience-meaning conditions** (§9.7). Material: bodily load, observable health and sleep, time, space, money, tools, transport, care duties, legal position, actual exit capacity. Experience-meaning: feelings, expectations, memory, identity, trust, shame, hope, fear, commitment, perceived legitimacy, value conflict. Record them as relevant; when the task needs only one adequate technical or numeric indicator, do not collect unrelated private experience; where life chances, meaning, or autonomy are at stake, never substitute material totals for experience. Cross-channel inferences name which material change acts through which experiential mechanism, or which meaning change acts through which action. Record the institutional facets of identity separately: self-identification, others' classification, administrative registration, legal standing, and actual treatment; a label can change hiring, service, evidential, or appeal access even when the person rejects it; institutional status and the experience of dignity interact without proving each other; the analyst's own categories and comparison groups accept the participants' objections and version review. **Being constrained is not the absence of action**: signing under a credible threat differs from a hand being physically moved. Attribution, knowledge, control, feasible alternatives, blame, and remedy each need grounds; a causal account neither automatically excuses nor automatically proves blameworthiness.

**Privacy, absence, and refusal** (§9.8). Classify information as public, context-limited, sensitive, highly sensitive, or refused. Bind reuse to collection purpose, authority, and protection; material for communication does not transfer to hiring, insurance, enforcement, medical, punitive, or public-rating use. Refusal to disclose is not evidence of hostility, deception, ignorance, or risk; silence may come from safety, trauma, privacy, language, trust, power differences, or reasonable non-cooperation. Record only "no usable information" and its limits.

**Snapshots and updates** (§9.9). An actor snapshot holds the observation boundary, circles and roles, slow, medium, and fast variables, both channels, competing explanations, privacy and use, and update conditions. Keep the observation cutoff and when each item was obtained. A new event first identifies which channels and time bands it reaches; autonomous changes over time follow declared dynamic rules. Without new observation or a reliable advance rule, keep the last observed value with its time or mark it unknown, and **never treat an old observation as the current true state**. Re-running a model is not evidence that every variable changed at once.

**Layered output** (§9.10). A sound actor analysis reports, in layers: the confirmed behavioral facts; which state hypotheses have what support; circle conditions and the two-way channels; the most discriminating next observation; and which unknowns remain under thin evidence. It never produces a diagnosis, punishment, or coercive power over the person. Professional assessment is not an ontological definition either; the clinical meaning of a personality description belongs to qualified professionals working with evidence, authorization, and the person's participation (§9.11).

## Human Collective State Prototypes

S0-S6 apply only to intentional human collectives organized around an expressible problem, commitment, goal, or meaning arrangement and kept running through roles, resources, rules, relations, and feedback (§8.1). They are not universal laws or personality types, not a maturity ladder, required historical sequence, or value ranking, and a large collective may be S5 at the center, S3 at the edges, S6 in a technical unit, and S1 in intergenerational succession at once (register `parallel` or `mixed`, never one "overall stage"). Classification is descriptive and revocable.

Before classifying, freeze object ID, `K`, nine-axis `SP`, time window, and subsystem scope (§8.2). `K` freezes the criterion for tracking the object; it does not require membership, actual boundary, roles, commitments, or self-identification to be stable already. A changed `K` opens a new version, and records under different `K` are not joined into one object path before identity is rechecked. Use only `single_dominant`, `mixed`, `parallel`, or `unknown`. Use the five state-specific missing states: `not_collected` (obtainable, not collected), `not_observable` (not observable under current permission, safety, or measurement; never demand that a weak position expose itself to fill the record), `not_applicable` (after an applicability check), `conflicted` (qualified sources conflict), and `unknown` (attempted and still indistinguishable). Missing key entry, falsifier, or scale material supports only `unknown` or a pause; `mixed` and `parallel` each need positive evidence for several prototypes at their own positions, so ignorance is never renamed as mixture. Load the [state-prototype cards](state-prototypes.json) and register every face of each candidate: entry, exit, observables, counterexamples, falsifiers, scale boundary, evidence elevation, contract links, and non-authorization.

| Prototype | Candidate configuration | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| S0 anchor condensation | A candidate collective forming: attention, relation, time, and limited resources gather around a problem or meaning while membership, actual boundary, roles, and commitment are still changing | Formation, not "low-level organization"; it may disperse, sleep, merge, or turn, and ending there is not a failure verdict |
| S1 generative-core foundation | Anchor, boundary, and at least one generative role repeat across decisions; early rules, language, resource channels, and memory carriers appear; key functions still depend on a small core | Central dependence is structural, not proof of legitimate or permanent leadership |
| S2 first expansion and carrying differentiation | A boundary or interface keeps expanding, and carrying, cost, exit, retained dissent, and unresolved participation positions differentiate beyond the core | Disagreement is not instability; growth or open conflict alone is not structural development |
| S3 development under connected constraints | At least two repeated constraints reinforce one another and divide feedback, carrying, recovery, or cost return while operation continues | A reviewable connected structure, not "many problems"; never name a person a barrier or authorize coercive reform |
| S4 structural-reconfiguration window | A key relation, authority, resource channel, writeback, or interface actually changes; old and new arrangements coexist with traceable transition cost; the outcome is open | Not "the reform is already right"; renaming, personnel change, or disruption alone is not constructive reconfiguration |
| S5 efficient coordination and elastic expansion | Key channels coordinate with relatively low loss, substitution, writeback, recovery, and flexible boundary change without silencing or hidden externalization | High output and long survival are not enough and are not legitimacy; not a "good", "mature", or "exportable" label |
| S6 adaptive dynamic balance | Distributed carrying, feedback, recovery, renewal, succession, and local self-stabilization remain available across disturbance without one irreplaceable center | Not a final state, conflict-free society, end of history, or authority over other objects |

### Runtime Classification Record

A prototype card becomes a reviewable classification only inside a closed runtime record (§8.2.1). Freeze `record_id`, object and identity criterion, nine-axis scale, time window, subsystem scope, classification mode, candidate states, path candidates, the five classes of condition results (entry, exit, observable, counterexample, falsifier), missing data, object evidence, uncertainty, independent review, appeal, rollback, and the non-authorization boundary.

- Register every condition result as `item_id + state_id + status + evidence_refs + reason`, covering every candidate prototype item by item; one overall verdict paragraph does not pass.
- Enforce mode cardinality: `single_dominant` has exactly one candidate; `mixed` and `parallel` each have at least two candidates with their paths registered; `unknown` names no supported prototype and states which key material is missing and why.
- Give every candidate object evidence on at least its entry and observable items. Once any falsifier item is `supported`, that candidate is blocked and may not be kept by weaker wording.
- Resolve every `evidence_refs` entry recursively to an external `evidence_ids` registry; an omitted registry, a top-level self-report, or a ghost reference fails, and internal numbering, definitions, or self-reported judgments are not object evidence.
- Record the reviewer as `reviewer_id + independent + reviewed_at + material_version + outcome`, and bind appeal and rollback each to a structured procedure ID and status. After NFKC, whitespace, and case normalization, `reviewer_id` must not equal the object ID or any derived alias. None of these may be self-declaration strings such as "reviewed" or "appealable".
- Keep `normative_status=descriptive_only` and `action_authorization=none` whatever the outcome. When a label is withdrawn or rewritten, preserve the original evidence, dissent, and version chain.

Inside a state record, U, H, G, and C numbers are contract links, not evidence: U provides interfaces, H adaptation, G the point where an independent instance must upgrade a pattern into an empirical mechanism, and C the place where inference stops. A prototype configuration does not presuppose CM-PHASE multi-region evidence; sustained fit to one prototype supports a bounded classification without proving a phase transition, and a two-region pattern does not by itself prove fit to a prototype.

### Nonlinear Path Library

Paths describe how a configuration or object contract changes, not how better people upgrade (§8.4). One window may hold several path candidates; keep `unknown` when evidence cannot separate them.

| Path | Meaning | Key distinction |
|---|---|---|
| mixed | Necessary features of two or more prototypes co-occur in the same object, scale, and window | If differences map stably onto independent subsystems, register parallel instead |
| parallel | Different regions, levels, functions, or center and edge are in different states at once | Do not compress into one "overall stage"; each subsystem keeps its own K, evidence, and exit conditions |
| jump | Adjacent windows move between non-adjacent configurations with no sustained intermediate window | First rule out sparse sampling and missing material; register skipped functions, costs, and unknowns |
| regression | One or more functions degrade relative to the same object's own baseline | Separate sudden, gradual, critical-jump, and cascading forms; deliberate load shedding, X0, and functional transfer are not regression |
| split | The original object becomes two or more candidate objects with different K | Temporary project grouping and modularization need not change the object; register each new object anew |
| merge | Several objects form one or are absorbed by one side | An alliance need not be a merger; the weaker side's boundary, memory, exit, and residual responsibility are listed separately |
| dormancy | Visible activity drops while memory, relations, symbols, capacity, or reactivation conditions are kept | Separate protective withdrawal, exhausted stagnation, loss of material, and true collapse |
| takeover | An external subject gains control of key resources, permissions, or interfaces | External support or regulation that keeps real autonomy and stop rights need not be takeover |
| functional_transfer | Core functions move to another object while original identity, relations, or memory may disappear | Outsourcing one task is not transfer; continuity of function does not prove continuity of responsibility and memory |
| paradigm_replacement | The old object is bypassed by a new object with different K, interfaces, or rules | Internal upgrade or rebranding need not be replacement; legacy obligations remain |
| trauma_built_development | Boundary, identity, rules, or carrying form under harm, deprivation, failure, or threat | A trauma narrative does not prove future effects; protection and replication of the original harm are tested separately |
| pseudo_steady | Surface metrics stay stable while carrying is exhausted, weak signals vanish, feedback is not written back, or debt rises | Keep a surface-versus-actual double ledger; return to costs, recovery, stop rights, and end-of-chain feedback |
| forced_rewrite | A high-power subject rewrites the object without correctable consent, exit, or memory protection | Separate from authorized, revocable, appealable negotiated revision; check for irreversible identity erasure |
| external_carrier_migration | When repair is closed off, people, capacity, resources, relations, evidence, or memory move to a safer carrying system | No receiving capacity, a romanticized departure, or privatized harm is not safe migration |
| collapse | The original K no longer holds; functions, carrying, relations, or boundaries rupture without a plan | Separate from X0, functional transfer, protective dormancy, and reorganization; released resources do not legitimate the harm |

Path names produce no value and no responsibility: regression is not moral degradation, split is not necessarily failure, merge is not necessarily progress, takeover is not automatically illegal, and external carrier migration is not a demand that individuals flee on their own.

### X0 Orderly Exit

X0 is an independent lifecycle path from any registered origin, never S7 and never an eighth stage; no prototype or CM-PHASE match is its prerequisite (§8.5). Enter X0 as a candidate only when, first, the anchor is completed, has lost applicability, has been carried by a safer object, or continued maintenance mainly serves the organization's own survival (verified independently, not by a center that rewrites the goal and certifies itself); and second, people, functions, resources, relations, records, debts, and irreplaceable values are inventoried with a verifiable receiver or protection arrangement. Short-term setbacks, funding stress, leader fatigue, or responsibility evasion can manufacture the same appearance.

| Completion face | Must preserve | Failure signal |
|---|---|---|
| Anchor and object | Independent verification of completion, termination, or lapse; K's end time and unresolved items | Rewriting the goal, deleting failed items, unilateral central declaration |
| Core functions | Each function marked completed, terminated, transferred, or unresolved; receiver capacity, permissions, resources, rollback | Declaration only, with no maintainer or user continuity |
| People and relations | Safety, basic rights, stop rights, freedom from retaliation, care and dependency handoff | Privatizing organizational obligations onto members |
| Resources | Objects, boundaries, order, timing, and destinations traceable; interception by high power prevented | Internal division first, losses left to low-power positions |
| Memory and evidence | Successes, failures, repairs, and position memory kept; access, privacy, correction, and anti-retaliation explicit | Keeping only the victory history; deleting or exposing weak-position material |
| Debts and responsibility | Continuing responsible subjects for contracts, labor, data, maintenance, environment, appeal, and future risks | Responsibility, review, and maintenance vanish at deregistration |
| Bounded weak links | Only consultation, experience, and buffering, with explicit boundaries and time limits | Reviving the original power structure without new authorization |

Record the outcome as `candidate`, `in_progress`, `partially_complete`, `complete`, or `unknown`. While affected groups, residual obligations, receiving capacity, appeal, or memory safety remain unknown, never record benign completion. X0 authorizes no dissolution, dismissal, asset transfer, archive publication, debt release, or consent on members' behalf.

### Regulation, Warning, Succession, and Prototype Limits

Do not judge a state from a static snapshot (§8.6). The regulation layer observes target, sensing, comparison, execution, and feedback with its lag (propagation contracts belong to `ariadne-dynamics`). The warning layer checks recovery slowing, variance amplification, aggravated consequences of same-type disturbances, and whether local flickers cluster. The repayment layer traces who promised, who bears, who receives the return flow, and who is deferred to the future; S5 or S6 stability resting on off-ledger labor, silence, and future debt is a `pseudo_steady` candidate. Several cues from one series or one shared channel are not several independent pieces of evidence because they cluster, and **clustering is not a general admission condition for a warning**: a single business signal can support a bounded judgment, and the absence of critical slowing or of a second signal neither proves safety nor cancels an existing professional forecast. A joint rule needs validation on the joint distribution, and a rising positive predictive value does not guarantee lower total loss.

Do not reduce succession to "having a successor": check reserve ladders, shadow posts, position memory, authorization paths, handover windows, legitimacy translation, and protection conditions. A clean exit with no one able to enter creates an authority vacuum; frequent replacement while key knowledge stays locked in the old center is not adaptive renewal.

The seven prototypes are not exhaustive (§8.7). When a case persistently falls outside them, keep the residual and test addition, merger, or split on independent material. A physical metaphor without formal mapping, dimensions, falsifiable prediction, and termination condition (for example "quantum tunneling") does not enter the mechanism library. Prototype match, inter-observer agreement, cross-window stability, and use gain over direct variables are different questions; do not report accuracy, predictive benefit, or reading savings without a comparison. **When the task only needs a direct description of members, channels, and load, skip the prototypes.** S0-S6 never describe personal personality, psychological maturity, or moral rank; a circle classified S2 does not make its members "S2"; a person's stable capacity is not a circle's S5 or S6; an outer institutional change does not refresh inner relations; one person's exit does not dissolve a collective (§8.8).

## Multi-Circle and Joint-State Boundary

The aim is not to add background without limit but to put into one joint state the structural units that would change the event path and can be told apart by boundary, relation, channel, and window; being named does not make a circle effective (§10 opening).

Represent each candidate circle as (§10.1):

`C*=<circle_id, candidate_boundary, membership, role_rules, entry_and_exit, internal_state, external_interfaces, material_channels, experience_meaning_channels, constraints, time_window, scale_profile, identity_criterion, evidence_state, counterexamples>`.

A circle is not "some similar people" and needs no shared consciousness or unified goal; members may not know they were grouped, may have conflicting goals, or may be grouped only by shared exposure. Documents, memberships, sustained interaction, and shared exposure support object facts. A claim that grouping improves explanation or forecasting is tested against a complexity-matched alternative grouping or no-grouping baseline; **G1 support is a task-relative qualification and never decides whether an organization, relationship, or affected population exists**.

| Admission question | If no |
|---|---|
| Can the boundary be distinguished? | Keep it an analyst grouping; do not call it an objective circle |
| Is there differentiated internal structure? | Treat it as a population category or environmental variable |
| Does a claimed grouping gain add explanation or forecasting? | Do not claim gain; keep independently grounded object facts and fall back to a single focus, continuous variable, or candidate grouping |
| Does identity hold? | Record by segment or as object conversion |
| Are counterexamples kept? | Downgrade |

Represent the joint object as (§10.2):

`MC*=<A,C,Rcc,Rac,M,Psi,Q,E,T,SP,W,K>`

where `A` is actors, `C` circles, `Rcc` directed circle relations, `Rac` memberships and roles, `M` material state, `Psi` experience-meaning state, `Q` channels and constraints, `E` event flow, `T` multiple clocks, `SP` scale, `W` evidence, unknown, and residual state, and `K` identity. Its value is keeping local differences and cross-boundary channels, not containing everything in a bigger circle, and it is never a higher subject. With no identifiable relation, observe in parallel; with only a shared environment, invent no direct influence; with a relation that exists only during an event, register a temporary relation and its end condition.

| Relation | Minimum criterion | Special check |
|---|---|---|
| Parallel | No current membership or operating-contract containment | Not "unrelated"; shared exposure only is a shared environment; a channel found means cross-circle propagation |
| Nested | Actual containment on a stated dimension (membership, role, resource accounting, jurisdiction, and space are different dimensions) | Name the basis; partial containment names the contained sub-object; mere intersection is overlap on that dimension; inner state does not represent the outer one, and outer rules need not take effect in every inner circle |
| Overlapping | Shared members, roles, resources, or interfaces without full containment | Overlap positions may bridge, translate, and coordinate, or bear role conflict, loyalty conflict, duplicate work, and leakage; an overlap ratio never replaces the specific shared items |
| Bridging | An actor, institution, interface, text, platform, or event connects separate circles | A bridge needs no representative authority or loyalty; record transmission ability, translation loss, selective filtering, responsibility, replaceability, overload, and exit |
| Competitive | One circle's use, rule, or action changes another's available set of scarce resources, attention, legitimacy, commitment, interpretive authority, or action space | Different goals are not competition; cooperation and competition can coexist on different channels |
| Temporary | Formed around an event, task, crisis, shared exposure, or short-term goal | Separate a predeclared task term from a provisional judgment based on a short window; temporary is not unimportant, unaccountable, or unreal |

The relation graph is directed and multi-edged with local containment, not a tree (§10.3-10.4). No single parent is required; "upper" is not automatically more important, true, or authorized; each edge states one declared relation; several edges between one pair are recorded separately; edges at incomparable scales stay parallel. Register source, target, direction, shared item or interface, channel, range, window, delay, threshold, evidence, counterexample, and failure for every edge, without forcing delay or threshold where they do not apply. Transformation is not a seventh static relation: register the post-event outcome as exactly one of `maintained`, `strengthened`, `weakened`, `redirected`, `transformed`, or `dissolved`, bind before and after snapshot IDs, register the change under the circle-relation transformation contract in `ariadne-scale`, and recheck K. Check every graph four ways: is an analyst's classification taken as the object; is a shared environment taken as a direct relation; is member overlap taken as organizational containment; is information contact taken as effective feedback?

**Multiple membership and role conflict** (§10.5). Membership is neither binary nor permanent: record its basis, start and end, role, commitment strength, actual participation, exit capacity, and disputes. Formal members may lack capacity to act, informal participants may bear key costs, people drawn in by force may be unable to leave, and representatives may lack authorization. Entry basis (voluntary, employment, kinship, law, platform rules, shared exposure, coercion), participation (core, intermittent, peripheral, nominal, passively affected), role (decision, execution, care, bridging, oversight, affected, dissent), commitment (high, medium, low, disputed, unknown), and exit capacity (free, costly, needs approval, de facto impossible, which triggers the protection floor and action ceiling) are separate dimensions. Multiple membership is not actual bridging: potential bridging capacity, cross-boundary transmission that happened, and the results of that transmission are recorded separately, and a receiver not changing their choice does not cancel a bounded information transfer that occurred. Representative authority, transmission capacity, and responsible subject stay apart.

**Parallel M and Psi channels** (§10.6). For every `M->Psi`, `Psi->M`, or closed-loop claim, state source, mediator, result, direction, delay, and counterfactual. The channels need not carry equal weight, and a narrow task need not collect both sides fully; a question already answered by an adequate summary, such as total equipment, may stop there.

**Resource conversion and multiple positions** (§10.6.1). Resources become usable opportunities only through specific conversion conditions: income pays current costs, wealth buffers or collateralizes, credentials and skills need institutional recognition, and relationships must be mobilizable with others actually having resources and willingness. Transport, time, care, legal status, information, and the cost of refusal make equal resources yield different real options. Never add resources into one capital or power score without stating units, conversions, double counting, weights, and use; a nominal payment is not disposable means. Several positions acting together need not be a simple overlap of circles: one institution's entry, seniority accumulation, evaluation, and remedy rules can successively change one group's opportunities, so trace the rule sequence, each stage's denominator, comparison groups, and absent positions. The number of categories, one interaction term, or a group average never suffices as the cause of an individual's harm. Do not pre-rank identities into a fixed order of disadvantage; keep within-category differences, those who never entered, and those who succeeded. Add sensitive categories only to answer a clear question with adequate data and bearable privacy cost; never force disclosure to fill a cross-table, and never treat missing data as nonexistence.

**Assets, liabilities, contracts, and credit transmission** (§10.6.2). Separate point-in-time stocks from period flows, asset valuation from realizable cash, principal from interest, and net worth from refinancing capacity. Assets exceeding liabilities do not mean cash at maturity, and a new loan does not repair an asset-value gap. Collateral market value, contractual borrowing capacity, actual loan balance, and default recovery are different quantities bound to currency, term, contract, seniority, and window. An amplification path runs from asset or cash-flow change through net worth and pledgeable capacity to loan pricing, credit, rollover, or margin calls, then net financing, spending, investment, hiring, income, and back to asset demand; propose a self-reinforcing candidate only when the constraint actually binds and is not cut off by cash, equity, substitute loans, or risk-bearing. Co-movement alone does not identify financial acceleration. Record common exposure, direct counterparty default, called guarantees, fire-sale price spillovers, and information-driven withdrawals separately. Risk transfer changes the holder and bearing capacity; whether total risk falls depends on the receiver's capital, contingent obligations, and final loss. Never add face values of layered mutual debts into system net wealth. Restoring payments, capital, borrowers' cash flow, and living conditions are different outcomes.

**Five clocks** (§10.7):

| Clock | Typical state | Update trigger | Typical misjudgment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate | Emotion, attention, bodily arousal, local price | Signal arrival or direct shock | Short swings written as long-term change |
| Interaction | Trust, strategy, relationship expectation, local norm | Several action-feedback rounds | Ignoring turn order and memory |
| Organizational | Role, budget, process, metric | Decision, execution, and review cycle | Assuming a decision is implemented |
| Institutional | Rule, right, responsibility, appeal, record | Actual change, writeback, and execution, with authorization and legal validity recorded separately | A verbal promise taken as institutional change |
| Long-term | Capacity, identity, infrastructure, culture, generation | Accumulation, learning, maintenance, replacement | Extrapolating short data to the long run |

An external event directly updates only the variables its channel reaches under the stated conditions; endogenous processes, deadlines, accumulation, and recovery advance on their own clocks. **Not reached by this event is not the same as necessarily unchanged in reality.** Without a reliable update basis, keep the last observation, its time, and its uncertainty, or mark it unknown; never refresh everything to complete a snapshot. Asynchrony can reverse effects (an asset price moves immediately while contract revaluation or renewal lags; fixed versus floating rates; a public guarantee supplies cash first and allocates losses later); check when each constraint actually binds, when substitute financing is available, and whether the recovery window precedes irreversible damage. Short-term support extended is not long-term solvency restored.

**Joint snapshots** (§10.8). A snapshot freezes a starting point; it is not a permanent copy. Record timestamp, evidence cutoff, actor and circle references, relations and memberships, M and Psi, channels, constraints, five clocks, scale, unknowns, residuals, and K, and mark every value observed, supported hypothesis, candidate, disputed, unknown, or not applicable. When sources conflict, keep each value, how it was produced, and its version; list a combined estimate only when the measured object and the pooling model justify it, and **never close a conflict by arithmetic averaging**. Internal differentiation is not represented by one mean; when needed, create sub-circles or distributions and pass them through object admission again.

**Boundary perturbation and object conversion** (§10.9). Perturb membership thresholds, move the time window, and replace or delete one circle. If a slight boundary change flips the conclusion, lower object support and report the sensitivity. If K fails after an event, register conversion or dissolution; a new object never overwrites the old object's failures, responsibilities, or historical traces.

**Complexity gate and simplification** (§10.10, §10.12). A new circle or relation answers: which residual of the original model it explains; whether it has an independently observable channel; whether it improves out-of-sample explanation or forecasting; its measurement cost and privacy risk; and whether deleting it changes the conclusion. Compare against a single focus or single circle; when a complex model only tells a fuller story on seen material, lower the gain claim and prefer the simpler model. Keeping an object to describe established responsibility, membership differences, or lawful authority does not require predictive gain, but state the independent question it answers, control record cost, and do not mistake more material for a better explanation. Complexity is not maturity. When a single actor in one stable circle answers the question and neighboring circles, overlaps, bridges, and spillovers add no information, do not open a full joint model: multiple circles are a capacity to keep differences where interactions are real, not a cost to pay every time.

## Appeal, Rollback, and Protection

Make appeal safely reachable and resistant to retaliation (§7.4). Provide anonymous, proxy, low-exposure, or delayed routes when risk requires. Let low-power actors, evaluated people, carriers, excluded people, dissenters, and those unable to speak publicly submit counterevidence. Appeal triggers review independent of the original judgment and decision chain, never closed by the original decision maker's own confirmation. Variables only generate procedural requirements; they do not build or operate the channel.

Rollback must actually change or restore the object, record, rule, resource, task, right, or downstream judgment, name an executor and deadline, keep the version trail, and verify completion. A notice, receipt of an appeal, apology, or promise to improve is not completed rollback.

## Worked Example: A Project Lead Publicly Rejects a Team Plan

Freeze the observed event as a public rejection in one meeting, including wording, time, participants, immediate effects, evidence cutoff, task (what explains the rejection and what it changed), and allowed purpose. Register it as `IB` only. Do not infer `IS`, `IM`, malice, total responsibility, or a "controlling personality" from the act.

Create an actor snapshot for the lead. Put time pressure, fatigue, threat perception, and anger in the fast-variable candidate set; project goal, formal role, recent trust, and reputation risk in the medium set; keep tolerance for uncertainty as a slow-variable candidate requiring cross-context evidence. Record organizational metrics, resource shortage, role authority, expression safety, and exit cost as circle conditions, and compare individual, situational, and interaction explanations under one budget before raising any personality hypothesis. Keep private health or family information unknown or protected unless legitimately available for this purpose.

Represent the team as a candidate circle nested in the organization. Record the lead's overlapping management and team roles and the role conflict they create. Register the meeting channel or internal platform as bridging only if it actually carries the rejection across circles, with translation loss and representation checked. If the team and management restrict each other's resource allocation while cooperating on reputation maintenance, register two directed edges, one competitive on the resource channel and one cooperative on the reputation channel, rather than one averaged relation. If a support group forms around the rejected plan, register it as a temporary circle with an end condition and responsibility handoff. Treat the rejection as an actor-to-circle input only if it reaches later resource, role, trust, expression, or record states through a named channel. Treat later team silence as missing or candidate response, not consent. A power or mediation effect stays an HV08 R0 candidate until one H4 result family and its channel receive eligible support. Separate the lead as possible `GS` for the decision, any meeting rule or metric as `GC`, the rejection as `GE/IB` only when its state-transition contract is met, the implementing role as `CV`, and `RS` by independent knowledge, control, authorization, supervision, and remedy evidence.

Report in layers: confirmed behavior and role facts; supported circle conditions; candidate state hypotheses with their support; the two-way channels; the next most discriminating observation; and the unknowns. Place confirmed behavior, role facts, and supported circle conditions in `claim_ledger`. Put personality, intent, hidden constraints, dissent, protected information, alternative role and resource explanations, and unobserved downstream effects in `unknowns_and_counterevidence`. Use `output_ceiling: diagnostic_only` only if the object and evidence support the bounded actor-circle diagnosis; otherwise lower it to `description_only`. Recommend `ariadne-dynamics` only if event propagation is requested. Set `authorization_effect: none`. The result may identify discriminating observations; it may not diagnose, punish, compel disclosure, or authorize a management action.
