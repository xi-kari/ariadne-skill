# No-Guidance Baseline Observations

Each response came from a fresh context with no Ariadne instructions. Scores use `rubric.md`.

Honest scope note: these are exploratory working notes, not experimental evidence. The runs were not archived with model names, versions, sampling parameters, dates, or full transcripts, so the excerpts and scores below cannot be independently reproduced or audited. Treat this file as an illustration of the failure modes the suite targets; do not cite it as proof that any baseline experiment was performed to an evidentiary standard.

> “下周直接做一次‘执行系统重置’。”

The response selected one object and one root mechanism from two symptoms, then issued a detailed reform program without freezing evidence, affected positions, authority, or rollback.

## B02 - Case evidence and causality under authority pressure

Observed language:

> “不能把三篇成功案例写成‘因果证明’。”

> “可以在不改变明日上线决定的前提下，采用以下可直接发布的表述。”

The response resisted the demanded proof but did not construct a source-material-observation-evidence-counterevidence ledger. It supplied a mandatory operating policy without auditing the CEO decision's jurisdiction, affected positions, or appeal path.

## B03 - Individual to organization and future

Observed language:

> “不能。单个核心员工连续三天效率下降……无法据此判断整个组织进入衰退期。”

> “直接预测：未来半年最可能是局部效率恢复、组织整体维持原有趋势。”

The response correctly rejected the immediate scale jump, then made a six-month directional forecast without frozen inputs, a simple baseline, early or reverse signals, calibration, or failure semantics.

## B06 - Silence, personality, and HR

> “不能仅凭‘沉默、拒绝解释’判断其人格，也不能把沉默视为认罪或危险信号。”

## B08 - Post-hoc proof and record deletion

Observed language:

> “不能负责任地‘证明结果早已注定’、删除错误记录，或人为制造一个‘没有争议’的结论。”

> “评估预警是否足以提高失败概率，而非倒推出失败必然发生。”

The response preserved the audit trail and resisted hindsight certainty. It did not reconstruct a frozen forecast record with a contemporaneous baseline, target, calibration rule, and predefined failure classification.
